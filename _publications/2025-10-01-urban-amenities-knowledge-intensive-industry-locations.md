contribution_en: "This article develops a multi-scalar framework showing how urban amenities shape knowledge-intensive industry locations: metropolitan amenity patterns organize urban sectors and competitive advantages, while local amenity availability and diversity support placemaking and localized industrial attractiveness."
contribution_zh: "本文以武汉汽车产业为例，构建了一个多尺度框架来解释城市设施如何影响知识密集型产业区位：都市区尺度上的设施格局塑造城市板块与竞争优势，地方尺度上的设施可获得性和多样性则通过地方营造提升产业集聚吸引力。"
highlights_en:
  - "Moves amenity research from inter-city comparison to the finer intra-urban geography of knowledge-intensive industries."
  - "Distinguishes metropolitan-scale urban sectors from local-scale placemaking mechanisms."
  - "Shows that amenity diversity is consistently important, while the quantity of specific amenities matters differently across central, semi-core, and peripheral areas."
  - "Argues for context-specific planning: central districts need diversity and urban buzz, semi-core areas need institutional support, and peripheral areas need basic services and accessibility."
highlights_zh:
  - "将城市设施研究从城市之间的比较推进到城市内部的精细空间尺度，解释知识密集型产业在都市区内如何选择区位。"
  - "区分都市区尺度的城市板块分工与地方尺度的地方营造机制，强调二者共同塑造产业集聚。"
  - "发现设施多样性具有稳定影响，但不同类型设施的数量效应会随中心区、半核心区和外围地区而变化。"
  - "提出差异化规划思路：中心区应强化多样性与城市活力，半核心区应补强制度和创新支撑，外围地区则更需要基础公共服务与交通可达性。"
