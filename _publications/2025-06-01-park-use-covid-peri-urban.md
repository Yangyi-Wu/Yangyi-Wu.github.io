contribution_en: "This article examines park use equity during COVID-19 through a comparative urban and peri-urban framework. By combining objective accessibility and quality measures with residents' perceptions, demands, and changing visitation, it shows why peri-urban vulnerability cannot be captured by conventional park access metrics alone."
contribution_zh: "本文以盐湖城为案例，在城市与近郊比较框架下研究 COVID-19 疫情期间的公园使用公平性。研究将客观可达性与质量评价、居民主观感知、改善需求和公园访问变化结合起来，说明传统公园可达性指标难以完整揭示近郊社区的脆弱性。"
highlights_en:
  - "Compares urban center, west peri-urban, and south peri-urban communities rather than treating the city as one homogeneous unit."
  - "Integrates objective measures of park access and quality with residents' perceived access, quality concerns, and planning demands."
  - "Finds that southern peri-urban residents report optimistic access despite weaker objective accessibility, while west-east disparities in park quality remain clear."
  - "Shows that COVID-19 magnified vulnerabilities in western peri-urban communities, calling for targeted, resilience-oriented park planning."
highlights_zh:
  - "比较城市中心、西部近郊和南部近郊社区，而不是把整个城市视为均质空间。"
  - "将公园可达性和质量的客观评价，与居民的可达性感知、质量担忧和规划需求结合起来。"
  - "发现南部近郊居民虽然客观可达性较弱，却对公园可达性持较积极感知；与此同时，公园质量存在清晰的东西差异。"
  - "指出疫情放大了西部近郊社区的脆弱性，因此公园规划需要更精准地面向公平与韧性。"
