Associate Professor · School of Urban Design, Wuhan University

I study how spatial differences emerge within cities, and how they shape public services, firm locations and residents' opportunities.

My work draws on urban and economic geography, spatial econometrics, network analysis and multisource urban data to examine the social and economic processes behind urban spatial structure.
