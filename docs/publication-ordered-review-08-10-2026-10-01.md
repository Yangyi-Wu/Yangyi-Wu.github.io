## Scientific boundaries

- Jiangsu's conventional inequality decline after 2004 is not a uniform trend for every polarization measure. Persistent high-high and low-low spatial clustering is distinguished from index dispersion. Place mobility concerns relative development positions, not individual migration. Some mobility remains possible without overturning core-periphery structure.
- The regional graphic's spacing, neighboring tiles and ordering traces are hypothetical. A first engine draft introduced variable-height bars despite the specification; a targeted edit removed them. No index series, county ranks or empirical geography were invented.
- Wasatch Front morphological dispersion / multiple centers is distinguished from functional dominance by downtown Salt Lake City. Limited functional polycentricity is not evidence that planning has failed.
- Trip chains often pass through centers instead of remaining wholly inside them. Walking is relatively more common inside centers, while cars dominate region-wide. No claim that cycling or transit dominates centers was added.
- Several original passages give inconsistent center and sample totals. The graphic and new summary do not repeat a numerical total. Schematic nodes, edges and the activity sequence are not observed counts, locations, flows or household paths.
- Amenities/creativity retains its earlier theoretical comparison and findings. The accessibility illustration now shows one proximity segment rather than a transport network. The paper's registered-output proxies identify creative firms; they do not measure the full range of urban cultural creativity. Qualitative CBD-to-suburb rules are not fitted effect curves.
- No original map was freely redrawn. Generated geographic motifs are conceptual, with explicit bilingual captions. All assets used the built-in image-generation tool, not API/CLI fallback; the tool exposes no backend model identifier.
- Bibliographic metadata, homepage selected papers and member preferences, including Yin's reflection, are unchanged. Archives remain text-only and the dissertation has no figure.
