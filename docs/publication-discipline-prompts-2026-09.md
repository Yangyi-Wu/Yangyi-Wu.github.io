# Discipline-specific publication image prompts

Date: 2026-09-19. Mode: built-in image generation, one fresh composition per asset.
No external API, key, CLI fallback or exact model-version claim.

## Visual policy

Choose the drawing style by the figure's analytical purpose, not by whether the
paper mentions buildings, parks or planning. Hand sketch and watercolor are
reserved for actual design proposals, site design and spatial-design scenarios.
None of the six research summaries below is a design proposal.

- Geography: spatial patterns, networks, scale relationships and restrained
  cartographic symbols. Real maps must come from original paper figures.
- Spatial analysis: precise digital configurations and explicit analytical
  dimensions. Conceptual arrangements must not imply measured results.
- Institutions and theory: legible conceptual relations and comparative logic,
  not picturesque architecture or decorative scenes.
- Quantitative evidence: source-derived charts only. Never simulate coefficients,
  curves or ranked values to make the result look scientific.

All six outputs are conceptual. They introduce no national maps, real-city
boundaries, empirical flows or effect-size estimates. Their footer and site
caption state this. Colors and compositions can differ; scholarly legibility,
source fidelity and bilingual pairing remain consistent.
