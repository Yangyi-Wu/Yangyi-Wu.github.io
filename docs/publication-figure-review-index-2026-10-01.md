## Scientific and visual boundaries

Figures distinguish theoretical proposals, editorial research agendas, literature reviews and observed associations. Hypothetical maps, patches, networks, grids and plans are explicitly identified. No generated national boundary map is substituted for source geography; no fitted curve, regression magnitude, effect threshold, causal intervention or model validation is invented. Hand-drawn wash is limited to planning illustrations, not general geographic diagrams.

Some papers were checked from primary publisher abstracts/previews because their full PDF was unavailable. Their captions and batch notes state that limit. Details not verified from those sources are omitted. This review covers website explanatory graphics, not an independent replication of every statistical result.
