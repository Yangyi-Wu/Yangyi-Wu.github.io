I am an Associate Researcher (equivalent to Assistant Professor) in the School of Urban Design at Wuhan University. I received my Ph.D. in Geography from the University of Utah in 2020, following an M.S. in Geospatial Information Sciences from the University of Texas at Dallas and a B.S. in Surveying and Mapping Engineering from Wuhan University.  

My research focuses on urban and economic geography, particularly urban spatial restructuring, housing and neighborhood change, and spatial equity in sustainable development. I am interested in how urban amenities, green infrastructure, and governance innovations shape inequality and resilience across cities and regions.  

I currently serve as Managing Editor of *Applied Geography* and participate in academic communities such as the Urban Transformation and Innovation Research Committee of the China Urban Science Research Association.
